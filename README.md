# ResearchDesign

## Situation
Online apparel retailers like ZARA face high product return rates, often due to sizing and fit issues, which lead to logistical costs, environmental waste, and reduced customer loyalty. Virtual Fitting Room (VFR) technology—using Augmented Reality (AR) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) emerged as a potential solution to help customers visualize fit and make better purchase decisions.
Our research team sought to evaluate whether implementing VFR technology could reduce return rates and improve customer loyalty in ZARA’s e-commerce platform.


## Task
Design and executed a randomized controlled trial (RCT) to provide data-driven evidence on how VFR technology affects:
### Return Rate — whether customers return fewer products after using VFR.
### Customer Loyalty — measured by repurchase rate, frequency, and time to repurchase.
### We aimed to quantify the business and sustainability impact of VFR, targeting a 15% reduction in returns and a 10% boost in loyalty.


## Action
Research Design:
### Conducted an 8-month randomized controlled trial with ZARA’s online shoppers.
Participants were randomly assigned to:
#### Experimental group: used the VFR feature when shopping.
#### Control group: shopped using traditional online product displays.
#### Data Collection:
Collected six months of transaction data, including product category, price, return behavior, and repurchase activity.
Ensured data anonymity and compliance with privacy standards by encrypting customer data.
#### Statistical Analysis:
Applied two-sample proportion tests to assess differences in return and repurchase rates.
Used Poisson regression to model purchase frequency and Cox Proportional Hazards models to measure time-to-repurchase (hazard rate).
Conducted 1,000 simulation runs to verify statistical robustness and control false positive/negative rates.
#### Ethical & Operational Controls:
Maintained identical promotions for both groups to eliminate confounding variables.
Provided participants full transparency and voluntary participation incentives (e.g., coupons).

## Result
### Return Rates:
Customers using VFR experienced a ~10% lower return rate compared to those who didn’t.
### Customer Loyalty:
VFR users showed a 10% higher repurchase rate and ~19% greater purchase frequency.
The time-to-repurchase was significantly shorter (hazard ratio ≈ 1.9), meaning customers re-engaged nearly twice as fast.
### Business & Sustainability Impact:
Insights support integrating VFR as a permanent feature to reduce reverse logistics costs and strengthen brand loyalty.
The approach aligns with sustainable retail goals, reducing waste and CO₂ emissions tied to returns.
### Broader Outcome:
The study provided evidence-based recommendations for scaling VFR technology in online retail, promoting eco-friendly practices and long-term customer retention.
